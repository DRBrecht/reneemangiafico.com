Case Study — April 2026

AI Strategy & Implementation Engagement

Regional Broadcast Media Organization  ·  South Jersey

A full-scope AI strategy and implementation engagement for a 40-year community broadcaster serving a regional cable and FM radio audience. The client needed to move from awareness to action — understanding which AI tools were actually worth adopting, what they would cost, how to implement them, and how to fund the transition. The engagement produced four original research documents and a nine-initiative scope of work totaling over 30,000 words of original analysis and planning.

What was delivered

AI Integration Research Report

Current-state analysis of AI tools purpose-built for community broadcasters — transcription, sports automation, ad production, radio automation, archive management, and newsletter infrastructure. Includes a full implementation stack under $400/month and revenue projections of $100K–$350K annually.

Nine-Initiative Scope of Work

Phased implementation plans for each initiative — including tasks, roles, tool budgets, success criteria, and risk mitigations. Covered ADA captioning compliance, sports highlight automation, AI ad production, a community newsletter, media archive digitization, mental health programming, arts partnerships, workforce development, and AI consulting as a revenue stream.

AI Social Media Strategy

Platform-by-platform strategy incorporating AI tools for clip automation, scheduling, and analytics. Included teen audience data analysis, ADA captioning compliance guidance, and a roadmap for reaching audiences beyond the client's existing Facebook presence — without adding staff.
